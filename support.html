Does DailyVet cost money?

No, all core services — AI doctor, feed plan, medicine finder — are completely free.

How long does vet verification take?

DLS or BVC license verification takes 24-48 hours.

What if prescription scan fails?

Take photo on flat surface in good light. If handwriting is unclear, try high resolution again.

Is service available in all districts?

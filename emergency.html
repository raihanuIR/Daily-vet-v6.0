Rapid First Aid

Poisoning

Keep calm. Don't force-feed. Note what it ate, go to vet.

Bloat (Cattle)

Keep standing with front legs raised. Don't let it lie down.

Severe Bleeding

Press clean cloth firmly on wound. Keep pressing.

Heat Stroke

Move to shade. Pour cool water on legs, ears, neck.
